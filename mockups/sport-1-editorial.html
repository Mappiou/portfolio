§ 04 — Passions / Movement

Of salt, ice &
long quiet trails.

Four disciplines, four cadences. Beach, rink, court, ridge. A short field-guide to the bodies of work that happen when the laptop closes — written in sweat, breath, and very occasional falling down.

i.
The sand piece
No. 01 / IV

Beach Volley

Barceloneta · Est. 2019

Played for years — summer afternoons on Barceloneta, the net tilted slightly toward the sea. What I love is the rhythm: serve, set, spike, the slow walk back to the line while the sun fades behind the W.

No. 02 / IV

Patinage

FR · Since age 14

On the ice since I was a teenager. It rewards precision and silence — the blade does most of the talking, and the rink empties of words the moment you push off.

No. 03 / IV

Badminton

Indoor · Weekly

Quick reflexes, short bursts of total intensity. Three minutes of a rally feel like a small lifetime — then the shuttle lands and everything is calm again.
